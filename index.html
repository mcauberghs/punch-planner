Today

How the timecard rounds: every punch moves to the nearest quarter hour (:00, :15, :30, :45).
  • 0–7 min after → rounds back (8:37 counts as 8:30)
  • 8–14 min after → rounds forward (8:38 counts as 8:45)
Punch IN in the first 7 minutes after a quarter hour. Punch OUT 8–14 minutes after. A real 30-minute lunch never loses you time.
Real = the time you really worked. Card = what the timecard counts.

Backup

Your punches are saved on this phone only. Updating the app never deletes them; uninstalling the app or clearing Chrome data does. Send backup opens the Android share menu: pick your email app and send it to yourself. The file always contains all your days, so the newest backup is all you need. The app reminds you every day when there are changes not backed up yet.
