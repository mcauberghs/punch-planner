How the timecard rounds

Every punch moves to the nearest quarter hour (:00, :15, :30, :45):

  • 0–7 min after → rounds back (8:37 counts as 8:30)
  • 8–14 min after → rounds forward (8:38 counts as 8:45)

So: punch IN in the first 7 minutes after a quarter hour, and punch OUT in the minutes 8–14 after. A real 30-minute lunch never loses you time.

Daily goal: 8:00 real work and 8.00 on the card. Lunch: at least 30 real minutes.
